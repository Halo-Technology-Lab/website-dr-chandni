What it is

Fresher, without looking done

As we age, the face naturally loses volume. Skin can start to look tired, hollow or loose, often around the cheeks and temples. Facial Rebalancing replaces that volume where it's been lost. Instead of focusing on one feature, Dr Chandni looks at how your whole face sits together. She uses a combination of dermal filler, biostimulators and other treatments where needed to lift and refresh it.

It starts with a consultation and skin analysis. Dr Chandni looks at the proportions of your face, where volume has been lost and what's causing the skin to loosen. The aim is a natural result, so you look like yourself, just fresher.

Good to know

Your questions, answered plainly

Is Facial Rebalancing right for me?

It tends to suit people who feel their face looks tired or has lost volume, and who want to look fresher without looking done. Often the area you think needs treatment isn't the one making the biggest difference. Dr Chandni will look at your whole face and explain what would help most.

What happens after I enquire?

A member of the clinic team calls you back for a free 15-minute chat. If a treatment could suit you, the next step is to meet your practitioner in person.

Who will I see?

A member of the clinic team will call you first. Your consultation and any treatment are carried out by Dr Chandni herself.

How much does it cost?

Facial Rebalancing is a combination treatment, priced from £700. The final price depends on what your face needs and is confirmed at your consultation. The in-clinic consultation costs £50 and is redeemable against your treatment.
