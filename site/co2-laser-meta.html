What it is, and what it is not

How CO2 laser resurfacing works

CO2 laser resurfacing uses the Cutera Secret PRO to work on the texture and tone of your skin.

It is a treatment your practitioner assesses in person. It is not a quick fix bought online, and it is not right for everyone, which is why it always begins with a proper conversation about your skin.

Good to know

Your questions, answered plainly

What does the Cutera Secret PRO do?

It is the device we use for CO2 laser resurfacing, which works on the texture and tone of the skin. Your practitioner explains what it can and cannot do for your skin at your consultation.

What should I expect afterwards?

Your practitioner talks you through what to expect during recovery, and what to do to look after your skin, as part of your in-person consultation.

Is CO2 laser resurfacing right for my skin?

It is used to work on the texture and tone of the skin. Whether it suits your skin is a decision for your practitioner, who assesses your skin in person before anything is planned.

What happens after I enquire?

A member of the clinic team calls you back for a free 15-minute chat. If the treatment could suit you, the next step is to meet your practitioner in person.

Who will I see?

Your consultation and any treatment are carried out by one of our qualified practitioners. We will confirm who you are seeing when we arrange your appointment.

How much does it cost?

CO2 laser resurfacing is £600 for the full face and £700 for the face and neck, with small areas from £300. The in-clinic consultation costs £50 and is redeemable against your treatment. Your practitioner confirms the full price with you at your consultation.
