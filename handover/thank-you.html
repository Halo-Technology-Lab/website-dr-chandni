How it works

A conversation first, never a hard sell

  1. 1

    Your free phone call

    When you enquire, a member of the clinic team calls you for a 15-minute chat. You can talk through what you would like to change and ask us anything you like, with no pressure and no obligation to take things further.

  2. 2

    An in-clinic consultation

    If a treatment could suit you, we invite you to meet your practitioner in person. This appointment costs £50, which is redeemable against the cost of any treatment you decide to go ahead with.

  3. 3

    Your treatment

    Treatment is only ever offered after an in-person consultation. Nothing is rushed and nothing is decided for you, so you only go ahead when you feel ready.

Before your call

Nothing to prepare

There is nothing you need to read or bring. The call is a conversation about what you would like to change, and it lasts about fifteen minutes.
